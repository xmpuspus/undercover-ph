# Alt text for the demo GIFs and videos

Each section describes a real recording of the live site. Four clips are from 2026-10-25, with "now" set to 9 AM on Sun, Oct 25. The sun-direction and rain clips are from 2026-10-22. Check the numbers against new frames after each new recording.

## walkthrough.gif and walkthrough.mp4

This is a real recording of undercover.ph on a desktop screen. A numbered caption names each step, and a dot marks each tap. Shaded is tapped. Greenbelt is typed as the destination and Ayala MRT Station as the start. The time is set to Tomorrow at 3 PM, and three routes show on the 3D map.

The recommended walk is 83% shaded, 20 min and 1.3 km, with 3.3 min in sun. The shortest walk is 72% shaded, and the most shaded walk is 88% shaded. The Shortest tab, then the Recommended tab, opens. The pointer moves along the bar that shows what covers each part of the route.

Play runs the shadows from 9 AM to 1 PM, when the walk is 76% shaded. The step list opens with 11 steps. Navigate shows the turn "Turn right, then walk on the southeast side (on your right) of East Street". In Sheltered mode the app picks a walk that is 25% sheltered, 19 min and 1.2 km.

The map zooms out to the area outlines. The last caption reads "undercover.ph is free and needs no sign-up". The GIF plays the take 1.5 times faster than the 75-second MP4.

## walkthrough-tall.mp4

This is the same walk in the phone layout, full screen at 1080 x 1920. A caption sits on top of the app. The camera zooms in on each control that is tapped, then eases back out.

The recommended walk is 83% shaded, 20 min and 1.3 km, with 1.9 min less sun than the shortest walk. The map tilts to show the 3D buildings and their shade. The cover bar shows a stretch of tree shade, 10 m long. Play runs the shadows to 5 PM.

Steps, Navigate and Sheltered follow. Sheltered gives a walk that is 25% sheltered, 19 min and 1.2 km. The video plays the take 1.2 times faster.

## sun-direction.gif

This is a real recording of the same walk with ground shadows on. The play button steps the day from 8 AM to 4 PM. At 8 AM the sun is east-southeast at 30 degrees. At 4 PM it is west-southwest at 21 degrees.

The ground shadows of the buildings turn from pointing west to pointing east. The recommended route changes from 85% shaded at 8 AM to 92% shaded at 4 PM.

## rain.gif

This is a real recording of the 3 PM walk from Ayala MRT Station to Greenbelt. The walk starts in Shaded mode, 88% shaded. Then Sheltered is tapped, and the router picks a different route.

That route is 25% sheltered, 19 min and 1.2 km, with 14 min without a roof. Its cover is 26% roof and 74% no roof. The shortest route is 6% sheltered.

## run-loop.gif and run-loop.mp4

This is a real recording of Run a loop on a phone screen, full screen. A caption sits on top of the app, and the camera zooms in on each tap. Shaded, then Run a loop, is tapped, and 3 km is picked. Ayala Triangle Gardens is typed as the start at 9 AM.

Three loops show, and the map tilts to show them over the 3D buildings. The first loop is 2.8 km, 20 min and 74% shaded. It has 1 crossing with signals and 10 marked crossings. Its steps open, each in English with a Filipino line under it.

The time is set to Tomorrow at 5 AM, where the hour list starts at 4 AM. The new first loop is 3.0 km and 21 min. Its card says that shade does not apply before sunrise or after sunset. It has 2.3 of 3.0 km mapped as lit.

Mark as run saves the loop, and the panel reads "1 saved run on this phone". Other loops then gives a 3.0 km, 22 min loop on 89% new streets. The clip ends on the same free, no sign-up caption as the walkthrough.

The GIF plays the take 1.4 times faster, and the MP4 1.2 times faster.
