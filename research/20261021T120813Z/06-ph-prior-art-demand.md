# No Philippine shade or shelter walking router exists, and demand evidence is strong

Notes from the prior-art and demand search, saved on 2026-10-21.
"snippet" means only a search result was seen, not the page.

## Threats and seed data that change the design

- Android Authority found a hidden Google Maps "prefer shade" walking toggle in an APK teardown (2025-11, snippet).
- Source: https://www.androidauthority.com/google-maps-sun-shade-apk-teardown-3613515/. The toggle did not work for the reviewers.
- If Google ships it, sun-only routing loses its edge. Covered walkways and rain shelter stay a PH-only advantage.
- PAGASA launched iHeatMap in April 2025: gridded real-time heat index and hourly forecasts (snippet).
- iHeatMap source: https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/index.php/press-release/174.
- Makati covered network (circa 2019, snippet): about 2 km elevated, 4 km covered, 7 underpasses, 250,000 weekday users.
- Makati source: https://www.development.asia/case-study/transforming-business-district-walkable-space-private-financing. Network owner: MaCEA (macea.com.ph).

## Philippine prior art

- No PH shade, covered-walkway, or rain-safe walking router after 11 searches. [UNVERIFIED absence]
- Search terms: lilim, silong, silungan, lakad, covered walkway map, Sakay.ph, Walkable PH, Move As One, hackathons, theses.
- Gaps in the search: no dedicated Komyut or Waze search, no app store search, no Facebook search.
- Sakay.ph combines walking with jeepney, bus, and rail legs. It does not mention covered walkways.
- Pacer lists user routes in Manila, some tagged "No shade". Pacer is not a router.
- Pacer source: https://www.mypacer.com/routes/12842/walk-trail-quezon-city-philippines.
- inShade (iOS, https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779049186) claims 100+ countries but does not list the Philippines.
- Hackathon winners were not shade routers: Gabaik (Hack-A-Street UP Diliman), Tanaw (Hack the Climate Manila), PASAHero (October 2025).
- No thesis covers shaded walking routes in Metro Manila.
- Closest work: Ateneo walkability thesis (https://archium.ateneo.edu/theses-dissertations/871) and a UP Diliman intersection paper, March 2026 (snippet).

## Global prior art

- Undercover.gov.sg, 2026-02-03: 300+ unique users and 750+ routes in week one. About 95% chose the recommended route. [VERIFIED]
- Cool Walks Barcelona is web-only. Its mobile app target is 2028 (snippet).
- A 2026-06-25 news story covers ASU Cool Routes in Tempe, with an app target of 2027 (snippet).
- ASU source: https://www.azfamily.com/news/technology/2026/06/25/cool-routes-tool-maps-shadiest-walking-paths-asu-tempe-campus/.
- A 2026-07-09 story says Georgia Tech Cool Routes covers 10 US cities (snippet).
- HeiGIT shade routing launched in Heidelberg in summer 2024 (snippet).
- BrightMaps covers 25 US metros. AirPath covers Dubai and Abu Dhabi (snippets).
- No adoption numbers turned up for these tools.
