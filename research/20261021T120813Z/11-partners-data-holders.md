# MaCEA is the best first partner, and no open walkway layer exists for Metro Manila

Evidence labels: [VERIFIED] means the page was read. [UNVERIFIED] means a search snippet or recall. Page notes are in raw/11/web_notes.md.

## Findings in short

- No PH government or developer publishes a covered-walkway or pedestrian-network layer. [UNVERIFIED] 14 searches found none.
- MaCEA holds the most useful private network in Makati: about 2 km elevated, 4 km covered, 7 underpasses. [VERIFIED] (2019 ADB case study, read in raw/03).
- MMDA holds a footbridge list. A 2019 FOI request asked for it. The outcome is unknown. [UNVERIFIED]
- DOTr says it does not hold local pedestrian data. Its FOI replies point to LGUs. [UNVERIFIED]
- The best first ask is a short email to MaCEA for a segment list and a map.

## Top 5 partners for a one-person Makati v1

1. MaCEA. It holds the Makati CBD walkway design and records. Contact: macea@macea.com.ph [VERIFIED].
2. OSM Philippines community and UPRI YouthMappers. They can map the gaps. Contact: talk-ph@openstreetmap.org [VERIFIED].
3. Singapore Undercover team. It can share what worked. Contact: undercover@open.gov.sg [VERIFIED].
4. MMDA. It holds the footbridge list and plans. Contact: the MMDA page on foi.gov.ph [UNVERIFIED page, 403 to the reader].
5. Makati City Government. It may hold sidewalk and underpass records. Contact: makati.gov.ph/contact-us [VERIFIED page exists, text did not render].

Ranking logic: value for a Makati v1 divided by effort for one person. Ayala Land ranks sixth because no verified contact turned up.

## Single best first ask

Email macea@macea.com.ph. Ask for a list or map of covered walks, elevated walks, and underpasses in the Makati CBD, with opening hours. Offer credit and a correction channel. Make no promise of exclusivity.
